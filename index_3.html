How pace is figured. Season totals come from ESPN, cross-checked against NFL.com. Projection is the player's production per team game carried across all 17 games — so a player who missed time is projected on his team's schedule, not his own, and is flagged where that matters. "Borderline" means the projection lands within 15% of the number.

Alt lines. Slip 06 uses inclusive thresholds: 30+ TDs means 30 wins, where a 29.5 line would need 30 as well but can never push. Those legs are figured against the exact number.

The grey line under each leg is workload — pass attempts, carries and red-zone touches, targets, and share of the team's offensive snaps. Two games is a small sample, so volume often says more than the total does: a receiver on the field for most snaps and drawing targets has a path to his number even when the yards have not arrived yet, and one producing without the snaps does not.
